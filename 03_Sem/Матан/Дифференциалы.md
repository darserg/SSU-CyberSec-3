$$

#Математический_анализ
$$
d^nf(x_{1},\dots,x_{m}) = \left( \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_{1}}\cdot x_{1} + \dots \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_{m}}\cdot x_{m} \right)^n f
$$
$$
d^2 f(x, y) = \left( \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \cdot dx + \frac{\partial}{\partial y} \cdot dy \right)^2 f = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2}\cdot dx^2 + 2 \frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x \partial y}\cdot dxdy + \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2} \cdot dy^2
$$
