### CB, page 31, task 4
We will talk about abandoned water filter station in Saratov. This station, well-known as "English filters station" or **water supply complex number one**, look like a few buildings for employees on the Sokolovaya mountain of the station and have a massive underground with England filters system. It was put up at the middle of XIX century to filter a water for the city. Nowadays it's abandoned and can only tell about former greatness of the city, of the time when it had the most active development. This station is not really popular with the tourists or local people, because visiting of isn't really safe, so the government decided to close this area.
