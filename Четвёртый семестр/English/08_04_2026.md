### **p44-ex3**
Additional expressions for each group:

**Expressing disagreement**  
- "I'm not convinced."  
- "I see things differently."  
- "I'm not sure I agree with that."

**Making a concession**  
- "That's a fair point."  
- "You have a point there."  
- "I can see where you're coming from."

**Making a concession and counter-argument**  
- "While that's true, I think..."  
- "That may be the case, however..."  
- "Although I agree with you on that, I still believe..."

### **p44-ex4**
1. **Expressions you probably would not use in formal or professional situations:**  
   **"Oh, come on." and "Are you serious?"** – These are too informal and could sound dismissive or rude in professional settings.

2. **Expressions you could use in any situation:**  
   **"I see what you mean." and "I have to admit that"** – These are polite, respectful, and appropriate for both casual and formal contexts.
