# Принцип включения-исключения
![[Drawing 2026-02-26 12.42.44.excalidraw]]

$$
\begin{gathered}
|A_{1} \cup A_{2} \cup \dots \cup A_{n}| = \sum_{i=1}^n |A_{i}| - \sum_{1 \leq i\not= j \leq n} |A_{i} \cap A_{j}| + \sum_{1 \leq i \not= j \not= k \leq n} |A_{i} \cap A_{j} \cap A_{k}| - \dots \\
+ (-1)^n |A_{1}\cap A_{2} \cap \dots \cap A_{n}|
\end{gathered}
$$
